# ÉLAVÉNE — Instagram launch plan

## Positioning

**ÉLAVÉNE — ideas for a calmer, warmer home.**

A media account about interiors, décor and considered details in a warm Scandinavian, Japandi and quiet-luxury aesthetic. The main language is English; a concise Russian version follows in captions. Affiliate product edits are separated into **EU** and **Russia/CIS** availability.

First-month goal: establish visual recognition, earn saves and shares, and validate a format for later commercial integrations.

## Profile setup

**Name:** `Home decor & interior ideas | ÉLAVÉNE`

**Bio:**

```
Calmer, warmer homes
Interior • decor • considered details
EU & Russia/CIS finds ↓
```

**Bio link:** one landing page with `EU finds`, `Russia/CIS finds`, `Work with ÉLAVÉNE` and `About`.

**Highlights:** `Start here` · `Finds` · `Styling` · `EU` · `RU/CIS` · `Partners`.

**Pinned posts:**

1. What ÉLAVÉNE is.
2. “3 details for a calmer home.”
3. “Find of the week” format.

## Visual direction

- Palette: ivory `#F5F0E8`, sand `#DCCCB8`, taupe `#A99784`, oak `#815F45`, sage `#99A18B`, charcoal `#302F2C`.
- Materials: pale oak, travertine, linen, matte ceramic, wool and matte glass.
- Light: soft natural morning or early-evening light.
- Cover: 3–6 English words, highly legible type and no more than two colours.
- Avoid: impossible architecture, overly glossy AI surfaces, excess small décor and unapproved logos.

**Base prompt:**

```
Warm Scandinavian Japandi interior, quiet luxury details, natural oak, travertine, linen textiles, handmade ceramic, warm neutral palette, soft daylight, editorial interior photography, realistic material texture, lived-in but uncluttered, vertical 9:16, no people, no text, no logos
```

For product integrations append: `Accurately place the supplied product; preserve its exact shape, material, colour and proportions.`

## Commercial disclosure

Show a product as part of an interior solution—not as a banner. Mark each commercial placement appropriately: `Ad`, `Paid partnership` or `Affiliate link`, in addition to any applicable local requirements. Verify both link and availability separately for EU and Russia/CIS. AI imagery must not misrepresent an actual product.

## Cadence and first-month metrics

- Reels: Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Sunday.
- Carousel or still post: Tuesday and Saturday.
- Daily Stories: A/B poll, material of the day, Reel reshare, audience question and product link where applicable.
- Review weekly: reach, average retention, saves, shares, follows and bio-link visits.
- Publish 16 Reels, 6 static/carousel posts and Stories every day.
- Identify the three best themes by saves and two best treatments by retention.
- Do not buy followers or use paid promotion before testing 6–8 posts organically.
